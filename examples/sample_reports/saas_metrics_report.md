# SaaS Performance Business Insight Report

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Overall performance:** Total MRR trends upward across the year, led by Enterprise and Professional plans.
- **Main management issue:** Starter SMB churn rises sharply from March through July, creating retention pressure despite new customer acquisition.
- **Financial impact:** Expansion revenue grows in Enterprise, but Starter churn weakens the quality of customer growth.
- **Priority action:** Launch a targeted retention review for Starter SMB customers and monitor support ticket volume as an early warning signal.
- **Confidence / limitation:** High confidence for MRR, ARR, churn, expansion revenue, and support ticket patterns. Causes of churn remain hypotheses without customer feedback or usage data.

## 3. Key Insights with Evidence

### Enterprise drives the strongest recurring revenue growth
- **Finding:** Enterprise MRR grows steadily from 132,000.00 in January to 219,000.00 in December.
- **Evidence:** Enterprise ARR increases from 1,584,000.00 to 2,628,000.00.
- **Business implication:** Enterprise expansion and acquisition should remain a core growth motion.
- **Confidence level:** High confidence.

### Starter SMB churn is the main retention risk
- **Finding:** Starter churn increases through mid-year and peaks in July.
- **Evidence:** Starter churn rate rises from 2.50% in January to 6.80% in July.
- **Business implication:** Starter growth may be less durable unless churn is reduced.
- **Confidence level:** High confidence.

### Support ticket volume may be related to retention pressure
- **Finding:** Starter support tickets rise during the same period as churn pressure.
- **Evidence:** Starter support tickets increase from 210 in January to 415 in July.
- **Business implication:** Support load should be reviewed as a possible retention signal, not a confirmed churn cause.
- **Confidence level:** Medium confidence.

## 7. Data Limitations

- The sample is monthly and aggregated, so it does not show customer-level churn cohorts.
- The dataset does not include product usage, contract length, cancellation reason, or ticket category.
- Support tickets are a possible signal, not confirmed churn causation.
